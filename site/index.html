Private

It stays on your device

No account, no server, no analytics. Everything stays encrypted on your device, and you can add a passphrase to lock it so only you can open it. Where your data lives.

Legible

You can read and edit every line

The model is a list of plain-language claims, each with its evidence and a confidence level. Nothing is hidden; you confirm, edit, or delete anything.

Portable

Yours to take anywhere

The output is Markdown that works with any model, hosted or local. No lock-in. The interview and distiller prompts are open source.

Honest

Honest about what it is

It's a model of how you tend to decide and speak, built from what you told it, not a copy of your mind, and only as good as your answers. It will be wrong sometimes, so Test mode exists to find and fix that.
